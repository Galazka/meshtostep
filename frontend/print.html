Frequently asked questions

Is the price really automatic?

Yes. We calculate based on model volume → filament weight → print time → material cost. Margin and shipping are added. No negotiation needed.

Why are large models split into parts?

Standard FDM print beds are 250×250 mm (≈25×25 cm usable). Models larger than that are auto-split into parts, each priced independently.

Do you ship outside Poland?

Yes — EU (2–5 days) and worldwide via DHL/GLS/Poczta Polska. Shipping cost is calculated automatically by destination.

Can I pick up the print?

Yes, select "Pickup" in shipping — price becomes 0 zł, local only.

What filament do you use?

PLA, PETG, ASA, PA12-CF, TPU and more from top Polish suppliers (Extrudr, Filamentum, Oetiker). Full material table below.
