3. Stripe Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland). Card data is entered directly into Stripe forms - it never reaches our servers. We only receive payment ID, amount, and status. Stripe policy: stripe.com/privacy.

4. File and Data Retention

  • Mesh and STEP files: deleted 30 days after upload/conversion.
  • Share links: expire after 7 days.
  • Account data: until account deletion + 3 years (claims).
  • Accounting records (invoices): 5 years from end of tax year (legal duty).
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.
